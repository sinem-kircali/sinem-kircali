## Hi, I'm Sinem 👋

I work at the intersection of **data science, marketing analytics and operations research**. I have a background in Economics and Industrial Engineering (Koç University) and an MSc in Data Science & Marketing Analytics (Erasmus University Rotterdam).

I like projects that end in a decision, not just a metric: who to target, what price to set, what a model is worth in euros.

**Tools:** Python · R · SQL · scikit-learn · XGBoost · statsmodels · Gurobi · SimPy · Hugging Face / LLM APIs
